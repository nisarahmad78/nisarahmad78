## About Me

**Applied AI Engineer** — I design and build production-grade AI systems: LLM applications, RAG pipelines, and autonomous agents that solve real business problems.

- AI Engineer at **Excels Tech Solution LLC** (Lahore, Pakistan)
- 3+ years across ML engineering, Generative AI, and MLOps
- I take AI products from prototype to production: APIs, vector search, agent workflows, observability, and cost/latency optimization
- Open to freelance projects in LLMs, RAG, AI agents, and AI automation

## What I Build

- **RAG systems** — document ingestion, chunking, embeddings, vector search, and grounded answers with sources
- **AI agents** — LangGraph workflows with tools, memory, and human-in-the-loop control
- **LLM backends** — FastAPI services, streaming, rate limiting, caching, and usage/cost tracking
- **AI products end to end** — backend architecture, data pipelines, and clean frontends on top
